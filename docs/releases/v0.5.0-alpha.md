# Aperture v0.5.0-alpha

The biggest Aperture release yet: a full **Material 3 Expressive** redesign, a brand-new **phone companion**, and Aperture's first **desktop apps**.

## ✨ Expressive styling
- **New identity** — the filled Aperture mark with Expressive brand motion: a spring-driven spin with a restrained settle, and a six-sided cookie in the launch morph.
- **Themed launch** — the launch animation plays in your theme's splash colours; tonal, layered monochrome icons and a new purple-spectrum TV banner.
- **Outfit everywhere** — Outfit variable typography throughout the app.
- **Expressive playback** — a wavy progress indicator that flattens smoothly on pause, interruptible wave morphs on rapid play/pause, and a straight indicator while paused.
- **Spotlight home screen** and a sidebar with motion-token focus animation and a sliding, fading wordmark.
- **Ambient refresh** — artwork-aware branding, theme-aware logo roles (Material TV purple fallback), frosted screensaver cards, and logo/clock cards that stay together as text scales.
- **Smarter, faster theming** — precomputed artwork palettes, dynamic palettes that settle without root-level animations, and isolated theme fades.
- **Personalise from day one** — pick a theme during first-run setup, customise while your library scans, and preview real Ambient layouts while you do.

## 📱 Phone companion (new)
- Pair phones from onboarding or settings; the companion browses your library and controls playback.
- The TV can adopt your phone's theme; shared three-colour swatches and theme options.
- Recognises whether it's paired with a TV, Mac or PC.
- Shipped as its own signed APK and on the TV app's Play listing.

## 🖥️ Desktop apps (new)
- Windows (MSI/EXE), macOS (DMG) and Linux (deb, rpm, tarball, Flatpak, AUR), powered by libmpv.
- Mouse scrubbing like a web player, arrow-key focus, dark title bar, and headless-mpv scrubbing previews.

## 🎬 Playback
- On-device DTS and TrueHD decoding, with a passthrough setting.
- Next-episode countdown with continue playback.
- Clicking the timeline seeks, even with the preview showing.
- Many focus fixes around the player, quick menu, subtitles and returning from playback.

## 🔧 Other changes
- App ID migrated to `cc.xdan.aperture` (existing installs migrate).
- Official TMDB logo in attribution; Play distribution safeguards; signed APK builds in CI.
- Performance: retained library snapshots and show grouping off the main thread.
